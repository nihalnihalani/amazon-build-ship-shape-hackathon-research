## 1. Scope, method, evidence legend, limitations

### Evidence-quality legend (source types used in the tables)
| Label | Meaning | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| official_docs | Developer documentation, specifications, regulator or legal text | Verifies a capability or rule. Undated pages verify a capability, not a trend. |
| official_blog_or_press | Vendor newsroom, official blog, agency press release, official social account | The vendor's stated offering or plan. Not measured effectiveness or adoption. |
| vendor_marketing | Product pages, store listings, vendor comparison blogs | Stated offering; competitor-authored comparisons are biased and flagged. |
| vendor_help_center | Support or help-centre articles | Good for shipped behaviour and limits; often undated or "updated N ago". |
| news | Independent journalism or trade press | Independent unless it repeats a press release or a vendor-commissioned survey (flagged). |
| community_forum | Forums, issue trackers, community surveys | Anecdotes unless several independent sources agree. |
| user_review_or_social | App-store reviews, social posts | Anecdotes; vote counts noted where available. |
| github_third_party | Entrant or third-party repositories | Self-description by the author; activity is not proof of quality. |
| analyst_or_other | Surveys, research institutes, analysts, aggregators | Quality depends on stated n, sponsor and method, noted per row. |

Other labels used in prose: **(assumption)** = unverified premise we chose; **(estimate)** = a number we computed or guessed; **(inference)** = a conclusion drawn from verified facts, not stated by a source.

### Limitations
- Reddit cannot be read through Firecrawl ("we do not support this site"); Reddit evidence is title or snippet only and is never counted as read.
- GitHub counts are lower bounds: private repos, non-GitHub hosting and unlabelled repos are invisible; `total_count` fluctuates between calls; theme counts are regex tallies.
- Devpost shows 35,590 registrations (4 Oct, 02:40 IST); registrations are not submissions, and the project gallery is still unpublished.
- Several demand statistics are vendor-commissioned or industry-sponsored (Five9, Radial, CTAM, Security.org, SafeWise) or VC-published (Menlo). Each is flagged in place.
- Alexa+ Greetings adoption, Vega-stick Miracast support, Alexa+ support for MCP 2026-07-28, and Partner API exposure of Ring intelligent-feature outputs were not verified.
- No win probabilities or market-size claims are made anywhere in this file.

- **Concrete user need.** Stop re-explaining. A request should survive interruptions, device switches and one targeted clarifying question, and come back with a visible outcome ("done / planned / waiting for you"). Amazon itself now markets cross-device resume; the only consumer frustration data read (Five9 via No Jitter; Menlo's trust ranking) centres on misunderstanding and repeating oneself.
- **Implementation implication (18 build days).** Persist every task server-side with an explicit ID, status, pending questions and an action log, so state survives the stateless MCP model and can be shown on a second surface (web simulator, Fire TV, phone). Ask one clarifying question only when a required slot is ambiguous. Keep to one domain and 4 to 6 tools with deterministic constraint checks. Do not build generic cross-service breadth; Alexa+ already does calendar, email, shopping and booking.
- **Why it could be hype.** Most launches are gated betas (Spark Ultra-only, Siri AI English beta, Muse US). App-chart rank is not retention. Menlo shows agent use concentrated among paying power users while legacy voice-assistant use falls. Merchants block third-party agents (Amazon versus Muse). The organizer says ideas that are latent LLM capabilities or existing Alexa+ features score poorly (forum 45397).
- **Platform supply versus demonstrated demand.** Supply: very high and accelerating (Amazon 30 Sep, 1 Sep; Apple 14 Sep; Meta 8 Sep; OpenAI 10 and 29 Sep; Google 30 Sep). Demand: moderate and concentrated (Menlo 24% regular agent use among AI users). No reviewed source measures demand for cross-device resume specifically. The Alexa decline among AI users is a headwind for any Alexa+-framed pitch.
- **Frustration evidence.** Misunderstanding and repeating oneself (Five9 via No Jitter: vendor-commissioned survey, n=3,000, customer-service context). Accuracy 45%, trustworthiness 40% and privacy 36% outrank ease of use 32% (Menlo: survey with stated n, VC publisher). IJTS paper exists (academic; findings not read).

Label the anecdotal ones (delivery instructions, fitness paywalls, Vega spec complaints) as anecdotes in the write-up. ### 5.4 Fallback: FTV-4F Across the Room, Listener Edition

Same user, geometry, remote-only workflow, Vega app, relay, Polly prompts, a11y scaling and phrase bank as the primary, triggered at the Thu 8 Oct 20:00 IST gate only if the Vega app and relay are green but the Android Chrome AGC-off monotonicity test has failed on two phones or browsers (or no Android phone can be obtained). The far phone page becomes the listener's station: after each phrase the household listener at the far side presses Heard, Not heard or Repeat on the far phone, and "heard across the room: 7/10 vs 5/10 last week" becomes the headline metric; the sustained "ahh" is scored by phonation seconds from voice-activity detection (a duration AGC cannot corrupt); the arc stays, labelled "relative level, this session only". Rival threats it removes: the measurement-credibility attack (a technical judge dismissing relative dB from an uncalibrated phone whose gain controller may be doing the work), and the head-to-head with Loud and Clear's baseline-versus-now progress on dB, because the fallback no longer claims cross-session loudness. It keeps the listener loop, which no reviewed rival shows and which answers the MARS finding that the free apps are patient-only with "very subjective" progress. Rival threats it keeps or sharpens: Loud and Clear, Speak Up and Voicibel still do the arm's-length measurement loop for free; the rivals red team itself warned that a human-tapped "heard it" reads as a self-report anyone can press, so Tech drops to about 3; the karaoke phone-as-mic precedent is unchanged; and the far-side geometry remains the only structural claim, now supported by a human judgement rather than a sensor. About 80% reuse by hours (estimate); competitive, not stronger.

### 7.5 Caveats
- All figures are lower bounds: private repos, non-GitHub hosting and unlabelled repos are invisible; GitHub `total_count` fluctuates; the Ring and Vega queries used different phrasings from 3 Oct, so their deltas are not like-for-like; the theme probe covered 100 of 229 rows under loosely documented OR semantics.

- READMEs are entrant claims; activity (a push) is not progress; stars (max 3) carry no signal.
- The quality bar is high and AI-assisted: new entrants arrive on day one with CI, MCP 2025-11-25 badges, Lambda deployments and recorded demos (Raksha, contextcue, ask-along, hotseat); at least one (daybridge) discloses Codex authorship. A recurring weakness to exploit: many Alexa+ newcomers are pure browser simulations with no MCP server, and several admit missing AWS access.
- No win probability is implied by any count.
