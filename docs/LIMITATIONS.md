# Limitations: what is measured, what is fixture, what is pending

Status: built from `.build/evidence.jsonl` (EV-0001 to EV-0040), `.build/blockers.md`, `.build/task-graph.json` and the reruns recorded in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md), at integration commit `63af0ae`. Nothing here is a prediction. Where a number is a design constant it is called a **product assumption**.

Product framing: **Not a medical device. Does not diagnose or treat any condition.** The app shows a *relative digital level* at a phone microphone, compared with a quiet-room floor measured minutes earlier. It is not dB SPL, audibility, intelligibility, comprehension, voice identity or a clinical outcome, and no efficacy claim is made.

## 3. DSP residuals (recorded, not hidden)

Both come from the rework of `docs/adr/AMENDMENTS-REV2.md` ("Measured outcomes of the DSP rework") and `fixtures/dsp/`. The model for both is synthetic; real-room behaviour is the subject of T04, which has not run.

**A1-01, burst phase dependence.** The voice-activity rule accepts a segment only with at least 4 candidate frames and a run of 3. Bursts are swept over 40 sub-frame offsets (`fixtures/dsp/vad-phase-sweep.json`):

| Burst | At +10 dB over the floor | At +20 dB over the floor |
|---|---|---|
| 100 ms | never accepted (0 of 40) | never accepted (0 of 40) |
| 125 ms | 0 of 40 | 15 of 40 |
| 150 ms | 0 of 40 | 35 of 40 |
| 175 ms | 7 of 40 | 40 of 40 |
| 200 ms | **27 of 40** | 40 of 40 |
| 250 ms | 40 of 40 | 40 of 40 |

Consequence: "bursts of 100 ms or less are never accepted" and "250 ms or more is always accepted at +10 and +20 dB" hold; the amendment's wording "200 ms or more always" does **not** hold at +10 dB (the frame before the opening frame can never be a candidate). Bursts of 125 to 175 ms are phase- and level-dependent: do not read "150 ms is rejected" into the product. A cough of 200 ms or more, music, television speech or a second speaker can be accepted as activity: the rule is level-based, not a voice classifier.

**A1-03, false activity in noisy rooms.** Target: probability of at least one accepted segment in a silent 8 s window of at most 1 % for rooms that pass the quiet check. Seeded AR(1) noise, 10,000 valid trials per model (`fixtures/dsp/vad-ar1-noise.json`):

| Noise model (spread, correlation) | P(active) | Meets 1 % |
|---|---:|---|
| 2.0 dB, 0.9 | 0.03 % | yes |
| 2.5 dB, 0.5 | 0.00 % | yes |
| 2.5 dB, 0.8 | 0.45 % | yes |
| 3.0 dB, 0.8 | 4.43 % | **no** |
| 3.5 dB, 0.5 | 3.45 % | **no** |
| 3.5 dB, 0.9 | 16.46 % | **no** |

The 10 dB open threshold was kept (raising it would reject soft speakers before real data exists). Consequence: in the noisiest rooms the quiet check still accepts, and a silent window can be scored `below today's band` instead of `no_voice`. **A silent window being scored `reached` is very unlikely but possible.** The T16 security gate simulated it (finding N-05; unconditioned AR(1) model, silent 8 s window, floor equal to the mean, calibration margin 13 dB, 20,000 seeded trials per cell) and found a silent window scored `reached` in **0.015 %, 0.3 % and 2.3 %** of trials in the three noisiest admissible rooms (spread and correlation 2.5 dB and 0.9, 3.0 dB and 0.8, 3.5 dB and 0.9; the same model gives an accepted segment in 0.3 %, 2.3 % and 10.2 %). That model takes the floor as the true mean and does not estimate it from a quiet check. The repository's own simulation, which estimates the session floor from a 5 s quiet check that must pass (`docs/MEASUREMENT.md`, "Can a silent window be scored `reached`?"; `fixtures/dsp/vad-ar1-noise.json`, block `t16_comparison`), gives **higher** rates for the same three rooms: an accepted segment in 0.95 %, 4.4 % and 16.5 %, and a silent window scored `reached` in **0.04 %, 0.77 % and 4.8 %**. The two differ in the model, not in the code (with the review's assumption the same generator reproduces the review's figures within sampling error); nearly all of the increase comes from the floor being estimated a little too low, and the gate accepted the repository's figures as the better estimate. Both sets are synthetic: measured noise must replace them (T04). An earlier version of this section and of amendment A1-03 said a silent window "cannot become a false `reached`"; that was an over-claim, corrected here and by an erratum in `docs/adr/AMENDMENTS-REV2.md`. T04 revision 3 includes that negative case (N8: a silent speaker in the real quiet room, 10 windows) as a **reported value, not a gate**: its rate is set by the room, not the phone, so a phone can be `passed` with a nonzero N8 (residual R25). The three noisiest fixture models also pass the quiet check less often (90.2 %, 63.7 % and 69.6 % of attempts valid), so many such rooms are asked to retry instead.

Other measurement limits (see [MEASUREMENT.md](MEASUREMENT.md)): 50 ms frames quantise onsets and durations; a quiet voice that never gets 10 dB above the floor is `no_voice`, not a low score; browser AGC, echo and noise-suppression settings are a request and are reported, never assumed off; the pre-window check is a detection aid, not a guarantee against contamination by TV speech or VoiceView; the wake lock is a request.

## 4. Per-session only

The band is set by the person's own calibration in this room, with this phone in this spot, in this session. A new session sets a new floor and band. There is **no** cross-day loudness comparison, no automatic target increase and no day-to-day dB chart (ADR-0001 section 10). History shows counts and durations per day only, with explicit denominators, and a day with any incomplete session is flagged incomplete. A tone test passing (T04) would not enable cross-day comparison; that needs separate reproducibility evidence across days, placements and sessions and a new ADR.

## 5. "Unqualified phone" labelling

No phone has been qualified. The qualification state per phone description is `not_run`, `passed` or `failed` (A9-02), initially `not_run` everywhere. Until it is `passed` every band result on the TV carries "(unqualified phone)", Settings shows "Not yet qualified on this phone", and `failed` forces Listener Edition (the listener's Heard / Not heard becomes the primary outcome and band verdicts are hidden). The T04 qualification protocol went through three revisions, all on 2026-10-05. **Revision 2** (frozen at `270c9dc`, hashes `7059dd75...`, `6b7c339d...`, combined `3ba1fdff...d820`, EV-0030) was **rejected** by the independent security review (T16 gate, section 7): the analysis trusted caller-supplied nominal levels (N-08), missing or malformed negative-case results passed criterion C7 (N-09), trial validity was a caller flag (N-10), a `params` override could relax any criterion (N-11), `percentile.ts` was not hashed (N-13), numeric inputs were not checked as finite, and the N-05 negative case was missing. **Revision 3 is APPROVED** (approving commit `c935e48`, security review, EV-0047) and frozen: protocol `695f1cdd...b715`, `qualification.ts` `73b5e651...44b0`, `percentile.ts` `9845289d...78b0`, combined `b9b93100...9c66` (EV-0046; the hash is unchanged by filling in the approval block, and was reproduced at `6291c90`). It fixes all of the above: C3 requires exactly the three frozen levels (-30, -18, -6 dBFS) and the full planned list of 45 trial slots per stimulus, excluded attempts carry one of four closed reason codes and are counted, C7 passes only with N1 and N2 present exactly once and well-formed counts, the parameters cannot be overridden, and N8 is added as a reported value. **Revision 3 has not been executed**: no trial exists (B-002), and the **qualification-mode harness that will produce the trial list does not exist yet and needs its own review (T04A)** before the first trial. Residuals recorded with the approval: **R24** the exclusion reason is self-declared (a re-run attempt can be excluded as "disturbed" and the analysis cannot check the note against the live log: in the reviewer's simulation, re-running the 4 worst attempts per level raised the pass rate of an honest-scatter phone from 8 % to 75 %, which the harness must reduce by deriving reasons from the unmodified numeric traces and printing every exclusion), **R25** N8 is reported, not gated, and the informational finding N-15 (an inherited property name such as `constructor` passes the unknown-id check; it cannot change a gated result and is left alone because fixing it changes the hash). The hashes in the evidence log identify the approved revision 3 only. Passing would enable `relative_band` for one phone only; it never establishes dB SPL, audibility, comprehension or clinical meaning.
